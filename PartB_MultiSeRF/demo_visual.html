Multi-SeRF bucket routing — visual demo

Filtered vector search: nearest vectors to q where B ∈ [b_lo, b_hi]. The baseline searches one graph over everything and discards out-of-window results; Multi-SeRF partitions the data into K=16 buckets on B and searches only the buckets the window overlaps.

The dot geometry below is an illustrative sample. Every performance number on this page is taken unchanged from the recorded headline run (results_partB_main.json: n=5,000, dim=32, nq=100, K=16, QPS at recall ≥ 0.9); seed ranges are from results.md §9.

Honest notes: B is uniform in this run, so the equal-frequency buckets are also visually equal-width. The wide-window slowdown (0.65x at 25%, 0.43x at 50%) is real at n=5k — a query-adaptive router removes it (27.3x at 1% and 1.00x at 50%; results.md §10), and it fades with scale (§12).
